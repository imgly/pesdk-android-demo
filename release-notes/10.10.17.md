## 10.10.17

### Fixed
* Yield between render passes when an export is waiting for image data, preventing repeated temporary GPU buffer allocations while large source images are still loading.

- [PE.SDK] Fixed a JPEG export that could hang when saving a large photo before the editor preview had finished loading.

- [Gradle Plugin] Fixed automatic PE.SDK and VE.SDK module selection failing when reading licenses older than version 2.4 with a features array.

- [Gradle Plugin] Fixed PE.SDK and VE.SDK product names in license files being treated as unknown products.
